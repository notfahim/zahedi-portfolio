Layout: CSS Grid collage (grid-auto-flow: column -- not dense, varied grid-row
span per tile) — no JS masonry library, tiles vary in size/aspect for the
collage look, populated from photography.json.

Idle behavior: while no pointer is over the panel, it auto-pans slowly and
steadily, reversing direction at each end (ping-pong, not an infinite loop).
Disabled entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.

On hover: pointer entering the panel immediately pauses the auto-pan and
hands control to the visitor — real overflow-x scroll (wheel / trackpad /
drag / the scrollbar shown above all work). A photo click opens the photo
lightbox (see "photo lightbox.txt"). Auto-pan resumes a short delay after
the pointer leaves.

The panel opts out of the page's Lenis smooth-scroll (data-lenis-prevent)
so its nested horizontal scroll doesn't fight the page's vertical scroll.

No category filter in v1 — one continuous wall.
